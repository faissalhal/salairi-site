Income tax reform: what changes in 2027

  • The tax-free band rises from MAD 40,000 to MAD 60,000 of annual net taxable income.
  • The progressive scale is eased every year until 2030 (2027 draft finance bill).
  • Deductible professional expenses rise to 45% (capped at MAD 35,000 a year).

Frequently asked questions

Is Salairi free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by ads.

Is my information sent over the Internet?

No. Everything is computed on your phone: your salary and situation never leave the device.

Are the 2027-2030 scales final?

No. They are the 2027 draft finance bill proposals; the app will be updated once the law is passed.

Does it cover the whole civil service?

The app covers the main corps and grades of the ministries (education, health, interior, national security, justice, finance…). Grades whose amounts are not published are flagged.

Does Salairi replace my payslip?

No. Results are estimates; only your official payslip is authoritative.
