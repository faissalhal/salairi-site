Morocco income tax scale: 2026 to 2030

The 2027 draft finance bill plans a gradual reform of employee income tax (IR). Here are the brackets in force in 2026 and those proposed up to 2030.

The tax-free band rises to MAD 60,000

In 2026, net taxable income is tax-free up to MAD 40,000 a year. From 2027, this threshold rises to MAD 60,000, about MAD 5,000 a month.

Professional expenses and dependants

  • Professional expenses: 35% in 2026 (25% above MAD 78,000 annual gross), 45% from 2027, capped at MAD 35,000 a year.
  • Dependants: MAD 600 per dependant per year, up to 6 people.

Example

For annual net taxable income of MAD 60,000 (MAD 5,000 a month) with no dependants: income tax is MAD 2,000 a year in 2026 (about MAD 167 a month) and MAD 0 from 2027.

The 2027-2030 scales are proposals and may change when the finance law is voted.
